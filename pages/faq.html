05 / Finding the Right Fit

Choosing an MMA Gym

Ask about coaching qualifications, beginner programs, class structure, safety standards, trial classes and the academy's training philosophy. Any gym that gets defensive about these questions is giving you the answer.

A good gym prioritises qualified coaching, safety, cleanliness, structured programs and a supportive community. Avoid gyms that rely on marketing claims without demonstrating actual expertise. Credentials should be documentable, not just claimed on a wall.

Look for documented coaching experience, competition experience, verifiable certifications and evidence of student development. At Redcrown MMA, Coach Aditya's BJJ lineage is traceable and his competition record is documented. Ask us and we'll show you. See the full coach profile.

Research reviews, visit the facility in person, meet the coaches and take a trial class before committing. Evaluate whether the environment matches your goals. If you're in Noida, Redcrown MMA's first class is always free, and there's no better way to decide than to walk in.

06 / The Arts

Programs & Disciplines

MMA combines techniques from multiple disciplines, striking, grappling and wrestling, into one system. Most traditional martial arts focus primarily on one discipline. MMA tests which techniques hold up when everything is in play at once.

MMA incorporates both striking and grappling. BJJ focuses specifically on ground fighting: submissions, positional control and escapes. BJJ is one of the core disciplines inside MMA, not a separate path. Both are taught at Redcrown. See our BJJ program.

MMA training commonly incorporates Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Muay Thai, Boxing and Wrestling, giving students a complete skill set across striking, grappling and ground. At Redcrown, all four are taught under one membership, alongside a full training gym. See all eight programs, or read why the gym matters even if MMA is your only goal.

Boxing focuses on punching, footwork and defence. Muay Thai adds elbows, knees and kicks, the most complete striking system. BJJ covers the ground. If you're unsure, start with Boxing or MMA. Both give you a foundation you'll use regardless of where you go next. Most serious students eventually train all three.
