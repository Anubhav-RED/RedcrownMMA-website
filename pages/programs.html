MMA

02 / Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

BJJ

All Levels — Gi & No-Gi

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu taught under a verified Blue Belt with traceable lineage — real positions, real escapes, real submissions, not internet-assembled curriculum.

Both Gi and No-Gi run year-round. Gi sharpens precision and grip control; No-Gi is faster and more scramble-heavy, translating directly into MMA.

Book Free Class

What You'll Train

  • Guard, mount, side control and back control fundamentals
  • Submissions and the escapes that counter them
  • Takedowns and throws relevant to ground entry
  • Live rolling — Gi and No-Gi sessions
  • Grip fighting and pressure control
  • No-Gi scrambles and transitions for MMA carryover
03 / Striking

Muay Thai

Zero to Fighter

The most complete striking system there is. Fists, elbows, knees and kicks, trained with the structure that builds timing, range control and fight-pace conditioning quickly — even with no prior striking background.

Pad work, partner drilling and conditioning rounds form the spine of every class, with technique broken down at a level a first-timer can follow.

Book Free Class

What You'll Train

  • Stance, footwork and range management
  • Punches, elbows, knees and kicks
  • Clinch fighting and control
  • Pad work combinations with a partner
  • Checks, blocks, parries and defensive footwork
  • Conditioning rounds built for fight pace
04 / Striking

Boxing

Beginners Welcome

Footwork, combinations, head movement and defence — boxing fundamentals sharpen everything else you train, which is exactly why it's a strong starting point if you're unsure where to begin.

Every session pairs technical breakdown with mitt work, so timing and combinations get built into muscle memory from week one.

Book Free Class

What You'll Train

  • Stance, guard and footwork basics
  • Jab, cross, hook and uppercut combinations
  • Head movement and slip-counter defence
  • Mitt work with a coach or partner
  • Ring IQ — distance, timing and pacing
  • Conditioning built around round-based work
