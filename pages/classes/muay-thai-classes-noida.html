About the Program

Muay Thai, the complete striking system.

Muay Thai, the national sport of Thailand, is the most comprehensive stand-up striking system in existence. It uses all eight points of contact, fists, elbows, knees and feet, along with clinch control and sweep techniques that make it devastatingly effective at close and medium range.

At Redcrown MMA in Noida Sector 18, Muay Thai is taught as both a standalone striking discipline and as the primary striking foundation for MMA. Sessions progress from fundamental technique to pad work, clinch drilling and controlled sparring.

What You'll Train
  • ▸Jab, cross, hook and uppercut combinations
  • ▸Teep (push kick) and roundhouse kicks: power and targeting
  • ▸Elbow strikes: horizontal, diagonal, spinning and upward
  • ▸Knee strikes: straight, diagonal, clinch knees
  • ▸Clinch control: entries, locks, sweeps and knees in the clinch
  • ▸Defensive structure: guard, head movement, checking kicks
  • ▸Pad work combinations: building timing and power output
  • ▸Controlled technical sparring at an appropriate level

Who This Is For

Muay Thai training for every level and goal.

Stand-up striking that is immediately practical, for fitness, self-defence or competition, Muay Thai delivers results faster than most martial arts.

🥋

For Complete Beginners

Muay Thai teaches usable striking from the very first session. Beginners learn stance, guard, basic punching and kicking before progressing to combinations and pad work. The technique-first approach means skills develop safely and stick longer.

🛡️

For Self Defence

Muay Thai range management, clinch control and striking power make it one of the most effective stand-up self-defence systems. Elbows and knees function in close quarters where punching is difficult, a critical practical advantage.

💪

For Fitness & Weight Loss

Muay Thai sessions are among the most intensive cardiovascular workouts in combat sports. Pad rounds, bag work and drilling build stamina, burn calories and develop total-body conditioning simultaneously. Weight loss and lean muscle development follow naturally.

⚔️

For Fighters & Competitors

Muay Thai is the standard striking base for MMA fighters globally. The clinch, elbows and knee skills transfer directly into the cage. Fighters at Redcrown use Muay Thai to develop competition-level striking under the same curriculum used by professionals.
