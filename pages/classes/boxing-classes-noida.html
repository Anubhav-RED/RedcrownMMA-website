About the Program

Boxing, the foundation of striking.

Boxing is the oldest and most refined striking art in competitive combat sports, and for good reason. The technical depth of punching, footwork, distance management and head movement produces skills that carry directly into every other striking discipline. At Redcrown MMA in Noida Sector 18, Boxing is taught both as a standalone program and as a core component of the MMA curriculum.

Whether you want to improve your general fitness, sharpen your stand-up game for MMA, or just learn to move and punch with real technique, Boxing sessions at Redcrown will build it from the ground up.

What You'll Train
  • ▸Stance, guard and weight distribution: foundational structure
  • ▸Jab, cross, hook, uppercut: mechanics and power generation
  • ▸Footwork: angles, pivots, lateral movement and distance control
  • ▸Head movement: slips, rolls, bobs and defensive reflexes
  • ▸Defensive structure: parries, blocks and shoulder rolls
  • ▸Combination drilling: linking punches into flowing sequences
  • ▸Bag work, pad rounds and mitts for timing and power
  • ▸Controlled technical sparring when you are ready
Quick Facts

Everything you need to know before you show up.

Who is this for?

Anyone chasing sharp striking fundamentals, whether the goal is fitness, self-defence, MMA or just learning to move and hit correctly.

What will I learn?

Stance and guard, jab-cross-hook-uppercut mechanics, footwork, head movement, defensive structure and combination drilling on pads and bags.

How long is a class?

About 60 minutes: warm-up, technical instruction, pad rounds and conditioning work.

What should I bring?

Just workout clothes and a water bottle for your first class. Hand wraps and gloves come once you're training regularly.

Do I need experience?

No. Every beginner starts with fundamentals, and technical sparring is optional, never mandatory.

Ready to start?

Your first class is free. No payment, no commitment.

Who This Is For

Boxing classes that deliver more than fitness.

Boxing is simultaneously the most accessible and most technical striking art. Every session builds skills you keep.

🥋

For Complete Beginners

Boxing is one of the best starting points for anyone new to martial arts. Stance, footwork and basic punching are straightforward to learn and immediately satisfying to execute. Progress is visible within the first few sessions.

🛡️

For Self Defence

Effective punching, footwork and the ability to manage distance are the most practical self-defence tools available in stand-up confrontations. Boxing builds composure and motor responses that function under real pressure.

💪

For Fitness & Weight Loss

Boxing training is one of the most effective conditioning formats available. Combination drilling, bag work and pad rounds simultaneously develop cardiovascular fitness, upper-body strength, coordination and agility. Calorie burn per session is substantial.

⚔️

For MMA Fighters

Clean boxing mechanics are the single most transferable striking skill in MMA. Sharp jabs, educated footwork and solid defensive structure separate fighters at every level. Boxing classes at Redcrown directly sharpen the stand-up game for cage competition.
