About the Program

Self Defence -- built for real situations.

Most self-defence programs teach techniques that work in demonstrations and fail under real stress. Redcrown MMA's Self Defence program is built differently -- drawn directly from Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai, two of the most proven combat systems in existence, and applied to the specific scenarios that actually occur: grabs, chokes, pushes, attacks from behind and threats in confined spaces.

Specific women's self-defence sessions are available, addressing the scenarios and situational contexts most relevant to women's safety. No prior experience is required for any format -- the program is explicitly designed as a starting point, not a progression from existing skills.

What You'll Train
  • --Wrist grabs, bear hugs and choke defences from standing
  • --Ground control and escape from mounted or pinned positions
  • --Distance management -- creating and maintaining safe space
  • --Basic strikes for creating escape opportunity -- not brawling
  • --Verbal de-escalation and awareness as primary defence tools
  • --Women's scenarios -- specific situations and high-probability attack patterns
  • --Practical drilling under realistic stress conditions
  • --Recognising and avoiding dangerous situations before contact occurs
Who This Is For

Self defence that works for your situation.

Practical training for people who want to feel safer -- not people preparing for competition. The skills are simple, direct and built to function under real pressure.

🥋

For Complete Beginners

The program requires no prior training. Every technique is taught from scratch with the assumption that you have never trained before. The focus is on simple, reliable responses to common attack patterns -- not complex sequences that require athletic ability.

👩

For Women

Dedicated women's self-defence sessions address specific scenarios -- grabs, harassment, attacks in confined spaces and situations where size disadvantage is a factor. Techniques drawn from BJJ and Muay Thai function without relying on matching attacker strength.

💪

For Fitness & Confidence

Self-defence training builds situational awareness, composure under stress and genuine physical confidence -- outcomes that carry into everyday life. The conditioning built through drilling also improves general fitness meaningfully.

🛡️

For Everyday Safety

The program is built around realistic scenarios, not competition rules. The goal is not to win a fight -- it is to create an opportunity to escape safely. That distinction shapes every technique and drill in the curriculum.
