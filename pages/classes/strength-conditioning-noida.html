About the Program

S&C -- the physical engine behind every technique.

Strength and Conditioning at Redcrown MMA is not a generic gym class rebranded for martial arts. It is purpose-built for combat sports performance -- developing the power, endurance, mobility and mental toughness that make techniques actually land, and keep you fighting in the final round when opponents fade.

The program is available to martial arts students who want to improve their physical capacity, and to people who want rigorous athletic conditioning without any contact or technique work. No martial arts background required.

What You'll Train
  • --Compound strength movements -- squats, deadlifts, pressing and pulling
  • --Power development -- explosive training for striking force and takedown power
  • --Cardiovascular conditioning -- intervals, circuits and sustained aerobic work
  • --Mobility and flexibility -- joint health, range of motion and injury prevention
  • --Core strength and stability -- rotational power and structural integrity
  • --Combat-specific drills -- movement patterns and energy system work relevant to fighting
  • --Recovery protocols -- cooling down, stretching and session management
