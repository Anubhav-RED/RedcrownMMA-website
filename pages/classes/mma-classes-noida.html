About the Program

MMA -- the complete combat system.

Mixed Martial Arts is not a single discipline -- it is the integration of striking, grappling, clinch, takedowns and ground fighting into one cohesive system. At Redcrown MMA in Noida Sector 18, the curriculum is structured so a complete beginner can start safely on day one, and a competitive fighter can continue developing every session.

Sessions blend technical instruction with live drilling. You learn a technique, drill it, then test it under controlled pressure. That feedback loop is what separates real martial arts training from exercise classes with punching bags.

What You'll Train
  • --Punching, kicking, knees and elbows -- standing striking fundamentals
  • --Takedowns, trips and takedown defence
  • --Clinch control, dirty boxing and wall work
  • --Ground control, positional transitions and submissions
  • --Defensive grappling -- escapes, reversals and guard play
  • --Controlled live sparring and rolling at a coached, progressive pace
  • --Basic fight IQ -- pacing, game-planning and reading opponents
