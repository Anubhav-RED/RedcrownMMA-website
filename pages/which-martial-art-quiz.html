Quick answers

There is no single best. Boxing and kickboxing are simple to start and fast for fitness, BJJ suits people who prefer technical, slower learning, and MMA combines everything. The best choice is the one you will keep attending.

Styles that train you against a resisting opponent, such as BJJ, Muay Thai and MMA, tend to transfer best. Regular practice with a coach matters more than the style label.
