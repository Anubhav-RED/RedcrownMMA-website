MMA Calorie
Calculator

A realistic range, not a made-up number.

Most online calculators give you one confident number. Real calorie burn varies by body weight, effort and what the class actually contains, so this tool gives a range and shows its assumptions.

Optional: add age, sex and height for a daily-needs comparison

How It Works

Calories per minute = (MET × 3.5 × body weight in kg) ÷ 200, multiplied by minutes trained. MET values come from the Compendium of Physical Activities, the reference researchers use. We use these entries: martial arts, slower pace, novice practice (5.3); kickboxing (7.3); boxing sparring (7.8); and martial arts, moderate pace, including judo, jujitsu and Muay Thai (10.3).

  • Technique-focused class: 5.3 to 7.3 MET for the whole session.
  • Typical class: 25% warm-up at 5.3, 58% technique at 5.3 to 7.3, and 17% conditioning at 7.8 to 10.3. That is roughly 15, 35 and 10 minutes of an hour.
  • Live sparring or rolling class: 7.8 to 10.3 MET.

The optional daily-needs figure uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for resting energy use, which is the standard estimate for adults.

What This Calculator Can't Know

MET values are group averages. Your fitness, technique efficiency, rest between rounds and the room's pace all move the real number. Wearables are also only estimates. Treat the range as a planning guide, and know that fat loss depends on your total calorie balance over weeks, not one class. This is general information, not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, check with a doctor before changing your training or diet.
