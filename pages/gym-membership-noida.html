About Gym Membership

A real gym, not a side offer.

Redcrown MMA started as a martial arts academy, and the mats are still the heart of it. But the gym floor sitting beside them was always built to the same standard, real racks, real plates, real equipment that gets used every day, not a marketing afterthought bolted onto an MMA brand.

Gym Only membership means exactly what it says. Full floor access, your own schedule, zero obligation to ever set foot on a mat. It costs the same as our MMA membership, because we're not interested in making the gym-only option feel like a downgrade.

Quick Facts

Everything you need to know before you join.

Who is this for?

Anyone who wants a serious gym without martial arts classes attached. No MMA background, no interest in it, no problem.

What's included?

Full access to the strength floor, free weights, racks and conditioning equipment, on your own schedule, separate from class timings.

How much does it cost?

Priced identically to MMA Only membership. From ₹4,499 per month, with better per-month rates on longer plans.

What should I bring?

Athletic wear, a water bottle, and a towel. Standard gym gear, nothing specialised.

Do I need experience?

None. Coaches on-site can guide your form in your first sessions if you want it, entirely optional.

Ready to start?

Your first session is free. No payment, no commitment.
