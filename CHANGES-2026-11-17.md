## Mobile "untouchable until scroll" (root causes)
1. `.coach-dock` / `.dock-speed-dial`: invisible 219x249px box over the page with pointer-events:auto swallowed taps. Now pointer-events:none except the button; closed items are visibility:hidden.
2. `mobile.css` forced `.mobile-book-bar` on (!important) while `style.css` hides it for the icon tab bar. Both rendered stacked; tab bar was dead underneath. Tab bar is now the only bottom nav.
3. Blog posts: inline `repeat(3,1fr)` "Keep Reading" grid stretched every post to 430–580px wide on phones (hamburger off-screen). Now 1 column on mobile.
4. Fixed nav sat on top of the ticker at scroll 0. It now parks under the ticker and glides up (js/main.js).
